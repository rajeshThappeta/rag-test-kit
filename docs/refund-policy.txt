Sample Academy Refund Policy

This document describes when a learner can get a refund for a course at Sample Academy.

Eligibility
A learner can request a full refund within 7 days of enrolment, as long as they have completed less than 20 percent of the course videos. After 7 days, no refund is given for self-paced courses.

Live cohort courses
Live cohort courses follow a different rule. A learner can cancel up to 3 days before the first live session and receive a full refund. Once the first live session has started, the learner can move to a later cohort one time, free of charge, but cannot get a refund.

How to request a refund
Send an email to the support team with your enrolment ID and the reason for the request. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 10 working days.

Exceptions
Courses bought at a discount during a sale can be refunded under the same rules, but the refund amount is the discounted price that was actually paid.
